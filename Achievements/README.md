## 📡 Research Publication & International Conference

* **IEEE ECICE 2024 — Researcher and Co-author:** Co-authored the proceeding paper **“Signal Enhancement and Interference Reduction with Minimum-Variance Distortionless Response Algorithm Using MATLAB and GNU Radio Simulations.”**
* The research was presented at the **2024 IEEE 6th Eurasia Conference on IoT, Communication and Engineering (IEEE ECICE 2024)** in Yunlin, Taiwan, on 15–17 November 2024.
* The paper was published in **MDPI Engineering Proceedings**, Volume 92, Issue 1, Article 73, on 16 May 2025.
* My contributions included software development, validation, formal analysis, data curation, technical writing, review, and visualization for the MVDR smart-antenna simulations.
* Official Publication: [View the MDPI Paper](https://doi.org/10.3390/engproc2025092073)
