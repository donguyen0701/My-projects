## 📡 Research Publication & International Conference

### IEEE ECICE 2024 | Researcher and Co-author

Co-authored the proceeding paper **“Signal Enhancement and Interference Reduction with Minimum-Variance Distortionless Response Algorithm Using MATLAB and GNU Radio Simulations.”**

The research was presented at the **2024 IEEE 6th Eurasia Conference on IoT, Communication and Engineering (IEEE ECICE 2024)** in Yunlin, Taiwan, from 15–17 November 2024.

The paper was subsequently published in **MDPI Engineering Proceedings**, Volume 92, Issue 1, Article 73, on 16 May 2025.

My contributions included software development, validation, formal analysis, data curation, technical writing, review, and visualization for the MVDR smart-antenna simulations.

* Official Publication: [View the MDPI Paper](https://doi.org/10.3390/engproc2025092073)
* Supporting Document: [View Conference or Publication Certificate](https://drive.google.com/file/d/18T5PsgcqldWGeNlBa0dpZB4MCI571k5b/view)

## 🖥️ VGU Career Fair 2025 | Selected Exhibited Project

As team leader, I led the development of a fully functional **8-bit computer built from scratch**. The project was selected for exhibition at **VGU Career Fair 2025** to demonstrate digital system design, computer architecture, and hands-on electronic assembly.

Before constructing the physical system, our team designed, tested, and simulated its architecture using Proteus. The completed computer included essential modules such as registers, RAM, an arithmetic logic unit, a program counter, and a clock system.

* Project Documentation: [View the 8-Bit Computer Project](../Project%20DSD%20-%208-bit%20Computer/README.md)
