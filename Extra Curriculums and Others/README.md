This folder highlights my leadership experience, community involvement, engineering activities, and additional technical training outside the formal curriculum of my **B.Sc. Electrical and Computer Engineering** program at the **Vietnamese-German University (VGU)**.

## 🤖 REC Soccerbot Competition Organizer

### Vietnamese-German University | January–February 2025

I helped organize a university-wide robot soccer competition for VGU students. Participants developed, programmed, and competed with their own soccer robots.

The competition was incorporated into the **Exploring Engineering** module for the 2024 ECE cohort, allowing students to apply engineering concepts through a practical and competitive activity.

My contributions included:

* Supporting event planning and competition preparation.
* Coordinating participating students and competition activities.
* Assisting teams with technical and organizational questions.
* Supporting the operation of the competition on the event day.
* Helping create an engaging environment for hands-on engineering education.

## 🏎️ VGU Soccerbot Competition

### Hardware Coordinator & Co-Coder | Third Place | 2023–2024

Before helping organize the later REC Soccerbot event, I participated in the VGU Soccerbot competition as the hardware coordinator and co-coder of my team.

* Built a wirelessly controlled robot soccer car using Arduino, motors, motor drivers, Bluetooth modules, and sensors.
* Designed the robot frame using AutoCAD.
* Simulated circuit connections using Proteus 8.
* Integrated the mechanical, electronic, and control components of the robot.
* Finished in **third place** with my team.
